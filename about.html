Who We Are

FixNear is a platform designed to connect people with local workers who provide different services. Our goal is to make finding and hiring workers easier, faster, and more convenient.

Why We Created FixNear

The idea behind FixNear came from a common problem: finding a reliable worker when someone needs help with everyday tasks. People often waste time searching for electricians, AC technicians, plumbers, delivery riders, and other service providers. We wanted to create one platform where people could find available workers without unnecessary effort.

Our Motive

Our main motive is to make everyday life easier by connecting customers with workers. We also want to create more job opportunities for skilled and hardworking people.

How Did We Get This Idea?

We noticed that many people struggle to find workers when they need urgent services. At the same time, many skilled workers are looking for opportunities to earn money. This inspired us to build FixNear, a platform that brings both sides together.

Benefits of FixNear

  • Save Time: Find workers without wasting time.
  • Easy Access: Request different services from one platform.
  • More Opportunities: Skilled workers get more chances to earn.
  • Simple Process: Submit a request and let workers accept it.
  • Convenience: Get services without unnecessary searching.

Our Vision

We aim to make FixNear a trusted platform where finding local services becomes simple, quick, and accessible for everyone.

Our Promise

We believe that everyone deserves easy access to services, and every skilled worker deserves an opportunity to grow. FixNear is a step towards making that possible.
